Frequently Asked Commercial Property Questions

Complete clarity regarding commercial rent, security deposits, agreement terms, legal compliance, and landlord listings in Gandhidham & Adipur.

Why is Gandhidham & Adipur considered the top commercial hub in Kutch?

Gandhidham and Adipur serve as the commercial capital of Kutch due to their proximity to Deendayal Port (Kandla) and Mundra SEZ. The region features major banking networks, shipping company headquarters, high footfall retail markets (Tagor Road, Sector 12), and direct highway connectivity via NH 41.

What types of commercial rental properties do you offer in Gandhidham & Adipur?

We specialize in high-footfall retail market shops, fully furnished & bare-shell corporate office space, high-visibility main market showrooms, and roadside commercial lease plots across Tagore Road, Main Market, Sector 1-12 in Gandhidham, and Station Road in Adipur.

What are the typical security deposit and brokerage terms for renting commercial spaces?

Security deposits usually range from 3 to 6 months of rent depending on the location and property type, with flexible rent negotiation and transparent fee structures for both tenants and landlords.

Can I customize or renovate the leased commercial office/shop space?

Yes. Non-structural interior modifications (such as glass partitions, false ceilings, flooring, painting, and AC ducting) are permitted with prior written approval from the landlord. Most landlords also offer a rent-free fit-out period (15 to 45 days) for interior setup.

What is the standard lease deed duration and rental agreement process in Gandhidham?

Commercial properties in Gandhidham & Adipur are typically leased under 11-month registered lease agreements or long-term 3-to-9 year corporate lease deeds with renewable escalation terms.

How do you assist commercial tenants and landlords with legal due diligence?

We handle 100% of title verification, property ownership checks, municipal tax clearance, tenant background verification, and formal registered lease deed drafting for smooth operations.

How do I list my shop, office, or commercial plot with Shreeji Property Consultant?

You can list your property by clicking the "List Property" button on our website, or by contacting our specialist directly. We perform a free property valuation and initiate corporate tenant matching immediately.

Is a registered lease agreement mandatory for commercial properties?

Under Indian law, lease agreements exceeding 11 months must be legally registered with the Sub-Registrar Office with proper stamp duty payment. For 11-month leases, a notarized commercial rent agreement is standard.

Who pays GST and municipal property taxes in a commercial lease?

Commercial rent attracts 18% GST if the landlord is GST-registered or under RCM for corporate tenants. Property taxes are generally paid by the property owner, unless specifically agreed upon in the lease contract.
